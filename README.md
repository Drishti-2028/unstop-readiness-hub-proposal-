# unstop-readiness-hub-proposal-
Product case study: a feature proposal for Unstop that turns existing prep content into a personal, deadline-driven readiness tracker
Readiness Hub is a product case study built around Unstop, India's largest platform connecting students to placement drives, company-specific prep content, and skill assessments. While Unstop already hosts the raw material students need to prepare — company prep guides, mock tests, mock interviews — it doesn't answer the one question every final-year student is silently asking: how many days do I have left before Company X's test, and how ready am I actually?

This proposal closes that gap. Readiness Hub is a lightweight, personal layer on top of content Unstop has already built — turning passive browsing into a daily habit anchored to real deadlines. It covers the problem and research behind it, the business case for why this is the right feature for Unstop right now, a full v1 feature scope with priority and effort tradeoffs, a readiness-score formula, company-to-topic requirement mapping across TCS, Deloitte, Accenture, EY, and KPMG, and a 5-screen working prototype designed to feel native to Unstop's existing app.
